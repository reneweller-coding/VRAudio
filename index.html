Five instruments that compose while they play

Noctuary, Ephemeris, Phosphene, Totality and Parhelion each write their music from a seed — the key, the form, every note — and synthesise it in real time. Nothing is played back from a recording: the same seed gives the same piece, and a new one gives a piece nobody has heard.

They share their build, their panel and the hands of a Meta Quest, follow a DAW or an Ableton Link session, send their notes out as MIDI and their stems to outputs of their own. Free software under the AGPL-3.0.

  • VST3 + standalone
  • Windows x64
  • macOS Apple Silicon (untested)
  • Meta Quest
  • MIDI out
  • Ableton Link
  • C++20 + JUCE

What they share

  • One panel. The header, the tabs, the keys, Undo and Redo, the right click on every control (MIDI learn, the default) and Help (F1) are the same in all five.
  • One keyboard. A MIDI keyboard plays any voice, replaces or layers the composer's notes, splits between two voices, locks to the scale and shapes the velocity — each option off until chosen.
  • With other software. MIDI out of every note as it sounds, a stereo output per stem in a DAW, and Ableton Link in the standalone: tempo, bars, start and stop.
  • One headset. A Meta Quest app per instrument and one grammar of hands for all of them.
  • Checked. Every function documented and checked by a test, a CI run on every push, and a sound check that measures every style's loudness against its last known render.
The demos are rendered by each instrument's own command-line renderer and nothing else; the videos' pictures are by KaleidoscopeEnhanced, cut to the music's own score cues. The instruments are free software (AGPL-3.0); their sources, manuals and releases are on GitHub.
